Twemoji Mozilla — bundled COLRv0 emoji font
============================================

File: Twemoji.Mozilla.ttf
Source: https://github.com/mozilla/twemoji-colr (release v0.7.0)

The font binary in this directory is the COLRv0 build produced by the
Mozilla `twemoji-colr` project. It composes the Twemoji glyph artwork
(originally created by Twitter, Inc., maintained by the Twemoji project)
with build tooling under licenses listed below.

inside the binary via the embedded `assets/` tree) and will surface in
the future `--license` CLI subcommand once that Work lands.
